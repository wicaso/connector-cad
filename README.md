# Connector CAD

A collection of 3D CAD models, assemblies, and technical drawings for various electrical and electronic connectors.

Models are created or reconstructed from physical measurements, reference images, and publicly available documentation. They are shared as useful references for personal projects, prototyping, repair, and design work.

## Contents

Models are organized into individual directories and may include:

- **STEP** – 3D models and assemblies
- **STL** – Mesh models for visualization and 3D printing
- **DWG / DXF** – 2D technical drawings
- **PDF** – Drawings, dimensions, and reference documentation

Not every model will include every format.

## Accuracy & Disclaimer

These models are independently created and are not official manufacturer CAD files unless explicitly stated.

While reasonable care is taken, dimensions, tolerances, and mechanical fitment are not guaranteed. Verify critical measurements before using any model for manufacturing or production.
